Church & Organization Admin Guide

For admins managing seats, invitations, and your roster.

What "admin" means here

An organization can have up to 4 administrators at once. Admins are the only people who can approve additional seats, send invitations to church members to join, and manage who's on the roster. This guide walks through everything an admin can do.

Getting connected

The first time you set things up, you'll see a "Connect Your Church / Organization" screen. It asks for:

  • An organization name (required). This, along with the postal code, is your church's identifier across all functions within Selah for Church.
  • A postal code (required).

Click "Connect My Church" and you become the organization's first admin. This step doesn't activate anything — adding Leadership seats will activate the organization and open access to the app.

Understanding 'seats'

There are two seat types:

  • Leadership — for pastors, staff, teachers, and other leaders.
  • Membership — for your congregation. Membership seats can only be purchased once you already have an active Leadership subscription — Membership seats are opened with Leadership subscription(s), never added on their own. Membership has a 10-seat minimum.

Seats work as a pool. You add a specific quantity, and seats are filled as members join through invitations. If the pool is full when someone signs up, they are automatically added to a waitlist and get promoted when a seat opens — when someone vacates voluntarily, is removed by an admin, or when additional seats are added.

Adding and adjusting seats

"Add Leadership seats" or "Add Membership seats" opens a popup where you set the quantity to be added, see the pro-rated cost, and click "Continue" to check out.

  • Increasing your seat count adds the seats and bills the prorated difference at that time. New seats are thus usable right away. The new seat rate (assuming a lower price threshold has been crossed) for all seats will be in effect on your next billing cycle.
  • Decreasing your seat count works differently. If all seats are occupied, you'll choose who steps down.
  • "Add Exchanges" adds a one-time top-off block of exchanges if high usage means you're running low this month. It's simply headroom for the rest of the month.

Inviting Leaders and Members

There are two invitation types:

  • Leadership invite — This is tied to one specific leader's email and expires in 14 days. You can generate a fresh one if it lapses before they activate.
  • Membership invite — This is a shared, reusable link for your whole congregation. A membership invitation expires in 90 days if it remains unused, but you can generate a fresh one and invite more members anytime.

From the Invitations section: pick Leadership or Membership, fill in an email if it's a Leadership invite, and click "Generate Invitation."

For members, copy the link and share it. You can revoke an invitation anytime before it's used — a used invite can't be revoked.

Managing your roster

"View roster" under either seat block shows everyone in that pool. Click "Actions" next to a person to open the action menu — every action requires a second confirmation click.

  • Make admin / Remove admin access — grants or revokes admin status. You can't demote the last remaining admin — promote someone else first.
  • Suspend / Reactivate — Suspend temporarily limits someone's usage, but the seat stays reserved for them and their data isn't touched. Use this for a temporary hold, not a permanent removal. Reactivate restores access instantly.
  • Remove from roster — This is a permanent removal and frees their seat for another member. Removal doesn't cut off their access entirely: they're moved to an individual account with a free trial and can decide to renew on their own if they'd like. This can't be undone from the dashboard.

Two guardrails apply throughout: you can't suspend or remove yourself (so you can't accidentally lock yourself out mid-action), and you can't remove the last remaining admin without promoting another first.

Congregation Topics

This is a privacy-protected view of what your congregation has actually been exploring in Selah and is meant to be a pastoral signal, not surveillance.

  • Church organizations only. Not available for other org types.
  • Membership activity only. Leadership activity is never included.
  • This is aggregated over a 30-day minimum. The dashboard doesn't activate until your organization is at least 30 days old, regardless of how much activity exists.
  • 5-person floor. A topic only appears after at least 5 different people have engaged with it. This is a hard rule, not a rounding choice — it keeps a small church's early adopters from ever being individually identifiable.

Topics show engagement over a rolling 90-day window compared to the previous 90 days, so you can see whether interest in a topic is trending up, down, or flat.

Recent Admin Activity

A running log of roster and admin actions on your organization — who was responsible for any removals, suspensions, promotions, or demotions, and when they occurred. It stores the most recent 200 actions. Useful for tracking responsible parties.
